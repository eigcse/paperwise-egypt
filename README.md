# Paperwise Egypt

A free IGCSE past-papers website: past papers, classified questions, timed practice and a 50-question quiz.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages
1. Create a free account at github.com and click **New repository**. Name it e.g. `paperwise-egypt`, set it to **Public**, and create it.
2. Click **Add file → Upload files**, drag in everything from this folder (all the .html, .css, .js files and the `classified` folder), then click **Commit changes**.
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Build and deployment", choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, and click **Save**.
4. Wait a minute or two. Your site will be at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/paperwise-egypt/`.

## Settings
Open `common.js` and change the two lines at the top:
- `PDF_BASE` — where the past-paper PDFs come from.
- `CLASSIFIED_BASE` — where your classified booklets are (the `classified/` folder by default).
Live site: https://epicgamescomm.github.io/paperwise-egypt/
